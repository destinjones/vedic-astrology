The explorer

Every part of your chart is a door.

North or South Indian style, across all 16 vargas. Click a planet and see its dignity, its lords, its aspects and where it lands in every divisional chart. Click a house, a nakshatra or the lagna and the reading follows you. You never hit a wall that asks for your card.

  • Retrograde, combust and vargottama flagged on the chart
  • Lahiri, True Chitra, Raman and KP ayanamsas
  • Yogas and doshas listed with the exact reason each one forms

The 3D sky

Hold your birth sky in your hands.

Nine planets rendered in Blender, each tethered to its exact sidereal degree on a golden nakshatra wheel. Spin it, zoom it, click a planet to open its reading. Rahu is an eclipse. Ketu is a smoking comet. Nothing else in your bookmarks looks like this.

Planet longitudes are calculated from your birth data. The starry backdrop is artwork, not a map of real stars.

The timeline

See your whole life as weather.

One line, from now to a lifetime, that blends your dashas with the transits over your Moon. Good stretches and hard stretches are found for you, and each one tells you why it is there. Sade Sati, every retrograde station, all the ashtakavarga tables: one screen, one click apart.

  • Maha, antar and pratyantar dashas, each scored with reasons
  • Mercury to Saturn retrogrades, with the houses they cross
  • Sade Sati phases, including retrograde re-entries

Tested against the sky

The test suite checks real events: the Mercury stations of April 2024, Saturn entering sidereal Pisces in March 2025, Jupiter entering Gemini in May 2025.

Your birth data stays home

The chart is worked out in your browser and saved only there. The one thing that leaves is the text you type in the place box, sent to a city lookup. You can skip it and enter coordinates by hand.
