Client intake link: under Saved charts, an astrologer makes a link (with their name, an optional email and a note) for a client. The client opens a new intake/ page, writes their questions and contact, fills in their birth details on a simplified form, and gets a reply link to send back by email, WhatsApp, the share sheet or copy and paste. Opening the reply link in the app, or pasting the client's message, shows the details and saves the chart, opens it in a tab named after the client, and puts each question and the contact in the client's notes and history. There is no server: both links carry everything after the #, which is never sent to a server.
