Readings and phrases: an astrologer's own reading templates and saved phrases, kept in the browser. Templates are their standard reading structures (career, marriage, a first reading, or their own), with sections that name the placements they cover; a reading for a client starts from one and is printed in the consultation PDF after the note. Saved phrases are the astrologer's write-ups filed under placements such as "Moon in Rohini", "7th house in Libra", "Lord of the 10th in the 1st" or a yoga, and the app offers them whenever the same placement comes up again: in a reading, in the consultation note, and under every planet, house, yoga and dosha note of the Life Chart Book, which can also fill its empty notes from them in one click. Any note can be saved as a new phrase. The library exports to one JSON file and imports by merging, newer edits winning.
