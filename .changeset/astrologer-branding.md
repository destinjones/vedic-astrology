Astrologer branding in one place: "Your branding" holds the astrologer's name, practice, tagline, website, email, phone, address and logo, kept in this browser only and empty until filled in. It is printed on the consultation export and the Life Chart Book, and (unless turned off) on the everyday PDF report and the yearly PDF too: on the cover or first page, and with the name and contact details at the foot of every page. The consultation export and the book editor share the same fields, and details saved by the old consultation export are moved over once.
