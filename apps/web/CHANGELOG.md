# @vedic/web

## 1.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 28a06e5: Astrologer branding in one place: "Your branding" holds the astrologer's name, practice, tagline, website, email, phone, address and logo, kept in this browser only and empty until filled in. It is printed on the consultation export and the Life Chart Book, and (unless turned off) on the everyday PDF report and the yearly PDF too: on the cover or first page, and with the name and contact details at the foot of every page. The consultation export and the book editor share the same fields, and details saved by the old consultation export are moved over once.
- 07e835a: Client intake link: under Saved charts, an astrologer makes a link (with their name, an optional email and a note) for a client. The client opens a new intake/ page, writes their questions and contact, fills in their birth details on a simplified form, and gets a reply link to send back by email, WhatsApp, the share sheet or copy and paste. Opening the reply link in the app, or pasting the client's message, shows the details and saves the chart, opens it in a tab named after the client, and puts each question and the contact in the client's notes and history. There is no server: both links carry everything after the #, which is never sent to a server.
- 359dda4: Add a client list (readings, follow-up dates, notes and last predictions for every client) and upcoming alerts (dasha changes, Saturn phases and returns, Jupiter and Rahu sign changes, follow-ups and predictions due), all kept in the browser and carried by the existing backup and sync.
- f6c05b6: Life Chart Book: a long, printable book of a chart for an astrologer to give a client, next to the PDF report and the consultation export (both unchanged). It has a cover, title page, dedication, an opening letter, "the heart of your chart", and chapters that teach each idea before reading it in the chart with its classical source: the language of the chart, the rising sign, the Moon and birth star, the grahas, houses, yogas, vargas, dashas with a lifetime wall chart, a five- or ten-year forecast year by year, the seasons ahead, remedies with numbered sources, the glossary and a sources chapter. The astrologer writes their own letters, key points and notes on any chapter, planet, house, yoga, dosha, remedy or year in an editor that keeps drafts per client in the browser and can save them to a file. A screen edition downloads as one PDF; a print edition downloads an A4 interior with 3 mm bleed, chapters on right-hand pages and ruled notes pages, plus a cover sheet with the spine, for print-on-demand services such as Lulu.
- 5f25499: Offline app: Jyotisha Explorer can be installed on a laptop, tablet or phone and works with no internet connection once it has been opened once. The service worker keeps the site's own pages, scripts, fonts, artwork, the Swiss Ephemeris and the built-in list of 25,000 towns; learn pages are kept once read. It never stores anything else: no other site's responses, no URLs with a query, and nothing a visitor types (birth details stay in localStorage and after the #). A new version waits until the reader presses Reload in a small notice, so an open page is never switched underneath them. The manifest now has PNG and maskable icons, shortcuts, and relative paths, and every page links it.
- 57b15c5: Add practice tools for astrologers, all kept in the browser: a prediction track record across every client (areas, hit rate, what to check now, printable record and CSV), a remedy follow-up plan per client with check-in dates and a printable checklist, and invoices and receipts built from saved packages, with a payment link field and a CSV for accounts. All of it goes into the backup file and joins cleanly on sync.
- aaceecc: Readings can now be written and fixed in plain YAML, with a style guide, and every change is checked: a snapshot of every reading for a sample chart, a yoga lint (citation, cancellation note and test chart for each yoga), a readability check and a coverage report. The first hand-written nakshatra reading, Moon in Rohini, replaces the composed one. The site gains a credits page and the engine's API reference, and `@vedic/engine` can be built as an npm package.
- 6da4bf2: Readings and phrases: an astrologer's own reading templates and saved phrases, kept in the browser. Templates are their standard reading structures (career, marriage, a first reading, or their own), with sections that name the placements they cover; a reading for a client starts from one and is printed in the consultation PDF after the note. Saved phrases are the astrologer's write-ups filed under placements such as "Moon in Rohini", "7th house in Libra", "Lord of the 10th in the 1st" or a yoga, and the app offers them whenever the same placement comes up again: in a reading, in the consultation note, and under every planet, house, yoga and dosha note of the Life Chart Book, which can also fill its empty notes from them in one click. Any note can be saved as a new phrase. The library exports to one JSON file and imports by merging, newer edits winning.
- 18314ca: Add session mode, a clean screen to share on a video call that shows the client the chart and the talking points you lined up, never your private notes or cues, either full screen or in a separate client window. Add a multi-chart dashboard that puts the D1, D9, D10 and the running dasha on one screen, with columns, panels, ready-made layouts and your own saved layouts.
- 093462f: Add Session prep for astrologers: a one-page client snapshot (top strengths and challenges, the running dasha and its theme, slow transits now, and flags such as Sade Sati, Kantaka or Ashtama Shani, Mangal dosha and an upcoming dasha change), talking points for career, marriage, money, health, children and home, and a question finder that turns a client's question into houses, karakas and timing windows. Everything is rule-based from the existing engine, with sources shown; no AI service is used.
- 1b2b536: Video tutorial and promo: a chaptered video tour of the whole app for astrologers, recorded from the real app with captions, plays on the landing page and inside the app. The app has a Tutorial button (and a Quick switch command) that opens a player with chapter links, a written transcript of each chapter, and the place you stopped. A small "How this works" link follows the section on screen and opens the tour at that chapter; it can be hidden. The landing-page hero has a 40-second muted promo that plays on a loop (not with reduced motion or Save-Data, and it can be paused). Videos are not kept for offline use. `npm run videos` re-records both from a production build (needs ffmpeg).

### Patch Changes
