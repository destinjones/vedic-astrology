Offline app: Jyotisha Explorer can be installed on a laptop, tablet or phone and works with no internet connection once it has been opened once. The service worker keeps the site's own pages, scripts, fonts, artwork, the Swiss Ephemeris and the built-in list of 25,000 towns; learn pages are kept once read. It never stores anything else: no other site's responses, no URLs with a query, and nothing a visitor types (birth details stay in localStorage and after the #). A new version waits until the reader presses Reload in a small notice, so an open page is never switched underneath them. The manifest now has PNG and maskable icons, shortcuts, and relative paths, and every page links it.
