          The classical texts

          The rules come from these works. The reading text on this site is our own writing, not a translation of them.

          • Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, attributed to the sage Parashara: dignities, aspects, divisional charts, yogas, Vimshottari dasha.
          • Brihat Jataka of Varahamihira: lunar yogas, Nabhasa yogas, Pravrajya.
          • Phaladeepika of Mantreswara: most of the named yogas and their cancellations.
          • Saravali of Kalyanavarma: the Mahapurusha yogas and much else.
          • Graha and Bhava Balas by B. V. Raman: the worked Shadbala example our strength calculations are tested against.
          • Tajika Neelakanthi of Neelakantha: the annual chart.
